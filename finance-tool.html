Import a bank statement

Export a CSV from your online banking, then drop it here. Works with any SA bank — you just tell it which columns are which (once per bank, then it remembers).
💡 How to get a CSV: In your banking app/website, open the account, choose a date range, and look for Download / Export → pick CSV (or Excel/OFX). FNB, Capitec, Investec, Discovery and TymeBank all offer this.
